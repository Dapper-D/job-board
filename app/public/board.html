        What’s new

        Updates keep your answers, jobs, statuses, documents and logs. The board restarts and reloads by itself (usually 1–3 minutes).

        How the AI is used

        The daily refresh uses your AI (your AI) to score new jobs and write each tailored resume and cover letter once; the PDFs are also saved in the kit’s documents folder. The buttons under “Tools” only run when you click, and saved results stay on the job so you never pay for the same draft twice. “Research company” and “Who to contact” run during the next refresh, with web search. Everything is stored on this computer.
