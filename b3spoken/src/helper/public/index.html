How matches are scored

Every job is compared with the resume and answers you gave in setup, out of 100 points: skills the posting names that are on your resume (45), how close the job title is to the roles you picked (30), whether the level fits your years of work (15) and pay against your goal (10). Open "How this score was worked out" on any job to see its points.

  • Strong match (70 and up): most of what they ask for is on your resume.
  • Good match (50–69): a fair share is; worth applying.
  • Long shot (30–49): some overlap, with real gaps. Apply if the job excites you.

Jobs under 30, or with a hard stop (pay below your floor, French you don't list, a scam sign), aren't shown.
